Pre-authorisation

Setting a monthly budget authorises the agent to act inside it. The budget is a hard limit: a start or an increase that does not fit is blocked, and an unreadable budget blocks rather than allows. Heating appliances (cooktop, oven) stay blocked too. The one exception is the explicit, audited override on the console.
