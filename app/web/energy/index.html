HOW A DECISION IS MADE

Decision loop

  1. 1 · SenseSensors push a full snapshot at 1 Hz into the energy ledger. kWh is integrated from instantaneous watts.
  2. 2 · RequestA user command or a fired energy risk becomes one decision request with device, actions and remaining budget.
  3. 3 · DecideThe LLM returns ALLOW / BLOCK / DELAY / OPTIMIZE as structured JSON. It is called on events only — never per tick.
  4. 4 · ValidateRules override any answer that would exceed the budget, light a hob, break the comfort band or schedule a past delay.
  5. 5 · ActAudited device execution, or a durable scheduled job. Every decision is written to the log below.
